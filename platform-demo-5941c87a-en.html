Hi Mette! I'm your Culturequest assistant. What can I help you with today?
What should I be aware of after the September survey?
One thing cannot wait: Operations.

It is not the level alone, it is the combination and the movement: the stress score has fallen three surveys in a row, perceived control is falling with it, and recognition has started to follow. That pattern matches the burnout combination in the research (high demands + low control), and the movement is accelerating. I would categorise the risk as elevated, up from moderate in August.

Confidence: high (31 responses in Operations). The pattern is concentrated among employees with 2-5 years of tenure.
What can we actually do about it?
Three things, in the order they will move the most:
1. Capacity review in Operations. Find 2-3 projects that can be postponed, and announce it to the whole team. The comments point to unclear priorities as the concrete friction.
2. A fixed 1:1 rhythm every fortnight. Perceived control rises when prioritisation happens together with the manager.
3. Visible recognition of the 2-5 year group. They are the ones driving the decline, and recognition is a stronger exit driver than pay.
Next step Shall I create a draft action plan and an item for your leadership meeting on Thursday?
Honestly, Jonas doesn't have time for a 1:1 every fortnight. What do we do then?
Fair objection. Then we scale down the format, not the rhythm: 20 minutes, the same three questions every time (what is pressing most right now, what can be postponed, what should I remove?). It is perceived control that needs to move, and it moves through predictability, not meeting length.

✦Summary

67 of 124 employees have taken part so far, which gives an early but useful picture. This summary is updated continuously, and the results should be interpreted with caution until the survey closes on 15 July.

Psychological safety and organisational purpose currently score best among the categories, suggesting that employees have a sense of direction and feel relatively safe expressing themselves. Stress and workload, on the other hand, show early signs of pressure. The pattern is clearest in the comments from Operations, where unclear priorities and urgent tasks recur across several responses.

Participation is lowest in Operations and Warehouse & Logistics. Consider a targeted reminder there, so the final results rest on a representative basis.

📈What is working well
  • Psychological safety: employees dare to voice their opinions in meetings.
  • Clear communication of direction and purpose across departments.
⚠Areas for improvement
  • Stress and workload: early signs of pressure, particularly in Operations.
  • Unclear priorities are mentioned repeatedly in the comments.
